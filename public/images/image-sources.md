## Generated floral cake

floral-cake.png and celebration-hero.png use the same image, generated with built-in image_gen on 2026-10-24 as an illustrative demo catalog image, not a photograph of an actual Aurora’s Cake product. Prompt: Photorealistic premium editorial bakery image of one two-tier ivory and subtle blush buttercream cake on an ivory pedestal, sparse pale pink/ivory sugar flowers with tiny muted leaves, cream seamless background, soft window light, portrait 4:5, fully visible cake and pedestal, no people/text/logos/watermarks.

`floral-signature.webp` is the optimized WebP export of the generated floral cake. The original PNG duplicates were omitted to keep page downloads small.
